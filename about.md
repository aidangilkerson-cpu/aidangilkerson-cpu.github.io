# About

I’m currently leading strategy and growth at Wardstone, a defense technology
startup developing a modular interceptor platform to address emerging air and
missile defense challenges.

Before Wardstone, I served as a U.S. Army officer and worked with the Defense
Innovation Unit (DIU) on international technology partnerships and defense
innovation. I’m also pursuing my MBA at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business.

I’m interested in the intersection of emerging technology, business, and
national security—especially how new technologies can solve complex problems
and reshape existing industries.
