# I work at the intersection of technology, business, and national security.

I currently lead strategy and growth at **Wardstone**, a defense technology
startup developing a modular interceptor platform to address emerging air and
missile defense challenges.

Previously, I served as a U.S. Army officer and worked with the Defense
Innovation Unit on international technology partnerships and defense
innovation. I’m pursuing my MBA at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business.

My interests lie in how emerging technologies can solve complex problems and
reshape existing industries.
